# ESG and sustainability writing

Use with [the writing workflow](../SKILL.md). Identify whether the deliverable supports sustainability disclosure, ESG due diligence or assurance readiness, and write for that decision reader. Disclosure writing explains material matters and supported performance; due diligence explains exposure and investment/transaction implications; readiness writing explains evidence and control gaps. None constitutes a human assurance opinion. State the selected framework, jurisdiction, entity/perimeter and period without inferring statutory applicability. Let the argument determine length and exhibits; omit generic ESG lectures, formula tutorials and arbitrary chart quotas.

## State what is material and why

Distinguish financial materiality from impacts on people and the environment according to the selected framework. Explain how each matter meets the chosen test; do not blend the perspectives into an unexplained composite score. Where IFRS S1 is selected, connect sustainability risks/opportunities to financial prospects and their governance, strategy, risk-management and performance evidence. Impact findings can remain important without a defensible monetary estimate; financial exposure does not automatically establish an impact conclusion.

Develop material issues through claim, reviewed evidence, counterargument, quantified implication and disposition. An expert reader should understand what management assumes, why the evidence supports or challenges it, and what follows. Distinguish a policy from implementation, a target from an achieved result and a disclosed metric from an independently supported measurement. Explain whether contrary operating records change the conclusion; source volume and polished disclosure are not measures of evidential strength.

## Preserve measurement meaning

Keep organizational boundaries, sites, value-chain scope, periods, exclusions and calculation basis visible where they affect interpretation. Explain changes in coverage, acquisitions, outsourcing or methods before attributing a trend to operational improvement. Show absolute emissions alongside relevant intensity and identify its denominator; lower intensity can coexist with growing total emissions. Distinguish Scopes 1, 2 and 3 and incomplete categories without implying that all data have equal quality.

Identify Scope 2 market-based and location-based results separately and explain the instruments and limitations supporting the market-based claim. Present gross emissions, offsets, removals and avoided emissions distinctly; do not imply cancellation or comparability that the supplied accounting basis does not establish. GHG Protocol inventory preparation is not verification. Use reviewed measurements and models rather than silently recalculating indicators or selecting the most favorable boundary.

## Show operational and financial consequences

Connect supported matters to concrete consequences: production interruption, energy and material cost, required capital expenditure, remediation, customer access, workforce continuity, supplier dependency or financing conditions. Explain timing, scenario, uncertainty and the economic driver behind any supplied quantitative range. Do not invent a fine, probability, cost-of-capital premium or valuation discount from a rating or target. Where a number cannot be supported, describe the exposure and the specific evidence needed to quantify it.

Weigh credible adverse evidence, including incidents, regulator findings, grievances and restatements, against management's mitigation case. Separate an allegation from a substantiated finding and absence of disclosed incidents from demonstrated absence of harm. Report material contradictions and incomplete populations directly. Preserve conditional conclusions when implementation budgets, controls or measurement records do not support the proposed assumption.

## Make the disposition actionable

For disclosure, specify supported claims and corrections or omissions requiring resolution. For due diligence, state implications for the decision and the evidence needed to reconsider them. For readiness, describe criteria, traceability, controls and unresolved estimation/evidence gaps. ISSA 5000 provides an assurance-engagement framework; readiness analysis is neither execution of that engagement nor proof of local adoption. Do not issue an assurance badge or imply practitioner approval.

Conclude with issue-specific research/remediation actions, responsible capability and conditions for resolving uncertainty. Preserve domain judgments and financial review status. This is an implemented analytical writing profile, not a new analyzer or renderer: native report-outfit currently has no `esg` template. Any future generic-block presentation integration must be described according to its actual implementation and verification.
