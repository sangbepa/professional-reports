# Financial due diligence writing

Use with [the writing workflow](../SKILL.md). Write for the expert making a transaction decision. Establish buyer-side or vendor purpose, acquired perimeter, access to records and the exact maturity of the evidence. A buyer report should identify defensible earnings and cash requirements, downside and unresolved claims. A vendor report should present a supportable case while confronting credible buyer objections. Public-data screening must not be presented as transaction-level FDD. Shape the argument to the transaction; avoid accounting lectures, formula tutorials and predetermined page or chart counts.

## Develop the decision argument

For each material issue connect claim, evidence, strongest counterargument, quantified implication and disposition. Lead with what the reader can conclude and what could change it. Cite the reviewed record or result location and explain why it supports the assumption, not merely how many sources were collected. Distinguish observed facts, management representations, analyst judgments and contractual terms. Use the opposing explanation seriously before concluding; a seller's confidence is not corroboration.

The disposition should make the next decision concrete: retain the supplied analytical treatment, dispute it, exclude an unsupported adjustment, request specified evidence, or carry a clearly conditional scenario. Acceptance in an analytical case is not buyer consent, contractual agreement or professional signoff. Use current domain-reviewed calculations; the writer evaluates their evidential support and communication, not approves the accounting method or changes the model invisibly.

## Explain quality of earnings

Present reported earnings and the reviewed adjustment bridge with consistent periods, signs and perimeter. Distinguish accepted, disputed and unsupported items and explain recurrence, replacement costs, offsetting income, realization and alternative treatments. Keep prospective improvements and buyer-specific synergies separate from maintainable historical earnings. An exceptional label or management adjustment schedule alone is insufficient support.

For material items show the supplied earnings effect, tax treatment, cash timing and potential consideration effect separately. Explain when a profit adjustment has no immediate cash effect or when an apparent add-back requires future spending. If quantification is incomplete, identify the missing evidence and affected conclusion instead of writing an invented zero. State price implications only under the actual supplied valuation and purchase-price mechanism; otherwise describe exposure or conditional sensitivity. Do not turn a multiple convention into agreed consideration.

## Explain working capital and claims on value

Show whether the proposed working-capital level reflects seasonality, growth, billing cycles, inventory quality and normal collection/payment behavior. Explain why the reviewed benchmark fits the operating cycle and how year-end actions or overdue balances undermine it. Distinguish recurring cash requirements from a temporary snapshot. Preserve uncertainty in a proposed peg when transaction definitions or data remain incomplete.

Discuss debt-like and cash-like items within the actual deal perimeter and supplied definitions. Identify restrictions, timing, ownership and recoverability of cash; distinguish financing from operating balances. Trace each material exposure through earnings, working capital, net debt and tax/cash effects so the reader can see whether it is counted twice. Do not assume a buyer's locked-box, completion-accounts, leakage or indemnity terms. If contractual classification remains open, show the competing treatments and their reviewed consequences without drafting legal conclusions.

## Close the analysis for the reader

Order issues by ability to change sustainable earnings, cash funding, consideration or the decision to proceed, not by the order files arrived. Use exhibits only where they clarify a bridge, seasonality, concentration or contested treatment. Keep units, evidence coverage and conditional status beside the implication. Finish each material issue with the remaining research task, owner or capability and evidence needed for resolution. Retain unresolved disagreement and access limits in the conclusion. Deliver a professional decision argument, not a checklist of sources or a falsely completed approval.
