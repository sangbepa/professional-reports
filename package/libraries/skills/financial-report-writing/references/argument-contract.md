# What an expert-facing argument must carry

Apply to consequential claims, not every descriptive sentence. Use the engagement's existing ledger to bind inputs, decisions, outputs and reviews. Keep the report's prose natural and the supporting record inspectable.

## Argument record

For each material issue retain:

- the reader's decision and proposed conclusion, including its strength and scope;
- observations/source locators and the calculation/model paths used;
- interpretation and the selected judgment, with the reason for choosing it;
- a credible alternative or adverse explanation, what discriminates between them, and its disposition;
- value, earnings, cash, contractual, disclosure or governance consequence as relevant;
- conditions that change the judgment, remaining limitations, and the next action or trigger;
- the current input/artifact versions and actual review outcome.

An argument can be short. Its evidential links cannot be replaced by a lengthy summary of facts. Record the explainable selection rationale; do not request or publish private chain-of-thought.

## Strength of the conclusion

Use a purpose-appropriate level, for example an observed fact, bounded scenario, selected valuation indication/range, transaction adjustment judgment, investment/board recommendation, disclosure assessment or documented event. A draft can be complete for a scenario mandate while insufficient for a recommendation. State that distinction in the actual headline and conclusion.

Positive review requires that the evidence, calibration and credible alternatives support the claimed level. Source correctness and numerical integrity are necessary for relevant claims but do not establish a maintainable margin, recurring EBITDA adjustment, transition plan, fair-value range or approved resolution. Judgment does not require certainty; it requires a defensible selection with proportionate uncertainty and consequence.

If unresolved evidence can materially reverse the conclusion and cannot be bounded, retain `research_required` or `analysis_required` and return the affected work. If only a narrower conclusion is justified, change the report's claim strength, not the underlying source label. A writer cannot close an independent finding by adding “conditional” while preserving the rejected recommendation.

## Writing acceptance

An expert reader should be able to answer:

1. What is being concluded, for which decision and scope?
2. What observations and economic/technical mechanism support it?
3. Why was this assumption/adjustment/interpretation selected over a credible alternative?
4. How much does the choice matter, and what would change it?
5. What action, further evidence or restricted conclusion follows?

Use these as review questions rather than a universal table of contents. Their applicability varies by purpose: minutes require faithful occurrence and resolution records, not fabricated counterfactual valuation arguments. Do not reward unnecessary sophistication or force quantitative effects where they cannot be supported.

## Separation of quality judgments

Keep source fidelity, calculation integrity, economic/technical adequacy, editorial usefulness and visual execution distinct. A single score cannot silently combine them. A design score describes the inspected competing designs; it is not a valuation-quality grade. Reviewers state coverage, current artifact hashes, material findings and actual limitations. An author response or schema validator does not provide independent substantive acceptance.

## Editorial example

Weak: “The business has strong prospects, but the valuation depends on assumptions.”

Stronger structure: “The selected case relies on [specific driver] reaching [defined state]. [Evidence] supports [part of this mechanism], while [counterevidence] limits [another part]. Relative to [credible alternative], this changes [decision metric] by [model-bound amount]. We therefore [adopt/reject/restrict] the case for [reason], subject to [specific condition].”

Replace brackets only with substantiated material. The sentence pattern is illustrative, not a required style; missing evidence is returned for work rather than invented to complete it.
