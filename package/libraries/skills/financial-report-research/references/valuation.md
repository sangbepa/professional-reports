# Valuation research profile

Research the economics capable of supporting the requested enterprise/equity conclusion. The financial model is the consequence of those choices, not their evidence.

## Questions that determine the work

Identify the economic units and how they create cash, the information set and value basis. Determine which few assumptions account for most of the decision uncertainty. Obtain relevant observations for those assumptions before filling an entire forecast grid. Use [industry lenses](industry-lenses.md) where appropriate.

For maintainable earnings, examine cycles/regimes, price and mix, volume/capacity, cost absorption, competitive responses and the persistence of unusual gains/costs. Link a historical-to-maintainable bridge to the selected forecast. A current peak or trough, management aspiration or broad AI narrative does not substantiate an exact long-term margin.

For reinvestment, investigate maintenance versus growth expenditure, capacity/utilization/yield, depreciation and useful lives, leases, cash timing and working capital. Explain how the transition and terminal economics fit. Under justified, compatible steady-state growth/return assumptions, `reinvestment rate = g / incremental ROIC` is dimensionless; the monetary net reinvestment amount is the matching-period NOPAT multiplied by that rate. Define growth, return, timing and units, and distinguish net reinvestment from gross expenditure. This is a consistency relationship, not evidence for the selected incremental return or capital burden.

For discount rates, establish currency/date/risk scope, regression/sample limitations, nonoperating asset exposure and capital structure. Distinguish source-supported market observations from selected ERP/beta/capital assumptions. Test alternative defensible constructions rather than adding risk premia to compensate for an unsupported forecast.

For the equity bridge, reconcile dates, legal-entity ownership, cash restrictions, financial subsidiaries, debt/leases, minority interests, investments, tax/pension claims and share classes. Research stock and funding movements together. Do not claim a point-in-time balance from a uniform cash rollforward proxy.

For market evidence, select peers by economics and metric definition; reconcile fiscal periods, normalized versus spot earnings, rights/control and capital conventions. Seek evidence that challenges the base case. A comps output using the same noncore DCF and bridge is partly dependent, not wholly independent corroboration. Reverse DCF is an inference under specified constraints, not the market's observed forecast.

## Handoff and completion

Return assumption cases with observations, calibration, competing choices, quantifiable effects and adoption/disposition; peer inclusion/exclusion and dependence; bridge issues; and what remains unknowable from current access. Route recalculations to `valuation-design`/model capabilities and retain input versions.

Conclude whether the evidence supports a selected valuation range, only conditional scenarios, or requires further material work. When methods materially disagree, commission a specific explanation/test and assess its consequence; do not automatically average or simply cite cyclicality and declare the disagreement resolved.

Professional reference: [KPMG valuation overview](https://kpmg.com/de/en/services/advisory/deal-advisory/buy/valuations.html) identifies decision purpose, business/market context and understandable assumptions as part of valuation. Its service description is not evidence for any issuer's forecast. Public expert reports can inform argument structure only after their different purpose, access and subject interest are understood.
