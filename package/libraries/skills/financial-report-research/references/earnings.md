# Earnings research profile

Apply the shared research evidence contract. This profile specializes the decision questions and evidence required for earnings work; it supplies neither an investment conclusion nor a presentation specification.

## Establish the coverage state and decision

Identify the issuer, reporting perimeter, accounting basis, fiscal period, audience, information cutoff and decision the research should support. Distinguish initiation of coverage from an update to existing coverage. For new coverage, establish a defensible baseline of business economics, segment measures, seasonality, accounting comparability and capital allocation. For a covered update, retrieve the recorded prior forecasts, assumptions and thesis with their versions and dates. An undocumented recollection is not the prior model.

Build a decision-question graph connecting the reader's decision to performance, persistence, cash conversion, forecast revisions and thesis tests. Each material question should identify the relevant evidence, calculations, competing explanations and what remains unresolved. Do not start with a predetermined rating, target price or desired surprise narrative.

## Preserve time and comparison bases

Record fiscal period dates, publication or earnings-release dates, and the research cutoff separately. Capture observation timestamps for forecast datasets, including timezone when release timing matters. A post-release forecast snapshot cannot establish the market's pre-release expectation. Identify restatements and retain the original versus revised comparison basis.

Keep issuer-reported actual results, management guidance, the analyst's recorded prior forecast and dated consensus in separate records. Actual results must come from the issuer's relevant disclosures, with document locators, units, currency, consolidation scope and reported versus adjusted definitions. Management commentary is an attributed explanation or forward-looking statement, not an actual result.

For consensus, record provider, observation date, fiscal period, metric definition, aggregation method and available contributor coverage. Establish comparability before calculating a variance. Missing or incomparable dated consensus means no beat/miss label; describe a variance against the recorded analyst forecast explicitly as a model variance. Guidance is neither consensus nor evidence of a reported outcome.

## Investigate drivers, persistence and revisions

Reconcile performance movements to supported business drivers: volume, price, mix, currency, acquisitions, capacity, input costs and operating expenditure. Separate an accounting bridge from a demonstrated operating cause. Evaluate recurrence using history and underlying activity; a management description of an adjustment as exceptional does not establish nonrecurrence.

Trace earnings into cash using operating cash flow, working-capital movements, noncash adjustments, capital expenditure and timing effects. Keep quarterly, year-to-date and annual measures distinct. Do not treat a balance-sheet movement automatically as a cash-flow contribution.

For updates, maintain old-to-new estimates by horizon and metric, identifying changed inputs, unchanged assumptions, calculation effects and remaining sensitivities. Explain whether evidence strengthens, weakens or leaves the thesis unresolved. For new coverage, distinguish newly established estimates from subsequent revisions; do not invent an earlier forecast or thesis.

## Deliver research records

Return the decision-question graph, source records, claim records, alternatives and material gaps under the shared contract. Link each claim to exact evidence and reproducible transformations; distinguish reported facts, attributed explanations, calculated results and analyst judgments. Alternatives must include credible competing driver or persistence explanations, with evidence that would discriminate between them. Record missing consensus, unreconciled definitions and absent prior forecasts as gaps, explaining their decision consequences.

Additional earnings calculation methods require supplied current reviewed content or a separately resolved capability. Its page, font and chart defaults cannot supersede explicit user constraints. Select evidence and exhibits for the decision; impose no fixed length or chart quota.
