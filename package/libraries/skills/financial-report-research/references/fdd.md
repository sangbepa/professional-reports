# Financial due diligence research

Use with [the research workflow](../SKILL.md). Research the transaction decision, not an abstract catalogue of accounting risks. Establish whether the assignment is buyer-side diligence, vendor diligence or preliminary public-data screening; identify the business perimeter, information cutoff, transaction stage and actual access to records. Buyer research tests downside and the seller's claims. Vendor research tests the defensibility of the sale narrative and anticipates credible buyer challenges. Neither role licenses selective omission. Describe public-data work as screening when transaction-level evidence is unavailable.

## Questions and independent evidence

Turn the proposed investment case into questions capable of changing a decision: which earnings persist, which customers or projects sustain them, what cash the business consumes, and which obligations transfer. Distinguish management's explanation from the researcher's judgment. Seek evidence that could falsify the preferred explanation, including contradictory periods, failed projects, subsequent collections, customer losses and repeated supposedly exceptional charges. Do not count repeated quotations of one management statement as independent corroboration.

Request records matched to the claim: monthly general ledger and trial balance, audited accounts, bank and cash records, customer invoices and credit notes, contracts and customer cohorts, project profitability, work-in-progress and completion records. Establish population coverage and reconciliation to the reported totals before accepting an attractive sample. Use the available evidence/extraction capability to acquire, freeze and normalize records; use `valuation-evidence` only where its contract fits the assignment. Record locators, versions, coverage and limitations; raw-record availability is not a completed accounting test or approval of domain calculations.

## Quality of earnings

Investigate each proposed adjustment at transaction, customer or project level where available. Ask what happened, why it is nonrecurring or maintainable, whether comparable charges recur elsewhere, and what replacement cost or omitted income offsets it. Examine cutoff, returns, rebates, capitalization, related parties and realization of accrued revenue when material to the thesis. Distinguish a supported normalization from prospective improvement, buyer-specific synergy and an unresolved management estimate.

Classify adjustments as accepted for the analytical case, disputed or unsupported, with affirmative and contrary evidence. Acceptance here is a research disposition, not buyer agreement or human signoff. Link each item to the domain team's reviewed earnings amount and separate tax, cash and possible price effects. Establish whether an effect is recurring, timing-related or contingent. Do not derive contract consideration from an EBITDA adjustment without the supplied valuation and contractual mechanism.

## Working capital and equity perimeter

Research monthly and seasonal net working capital through the relevant operating cycle. Test growth, billing milestones, inventory ageing, overdue receivables, supplier terms and temporary collection/payment actions. A year-end balance or simple average is not automatically a defensible peg. Ask which balances belong to operating working capital and whether historical movements support the proposed normal level.

Investigate debt-like and cash-like items within the actual acquired perimeter: financing, leases, factoring, restricted cash, overdue obligations, accrued bonuses, taxes and other relevant exposures. Classification follows facts and supplied transaction definitions, not a universal list. Track each item across earnings, working capital, net debt and cash-flow analyses to expose duplicated benefits or deductions. Do not infer a locked-box, completion-accounts or other buyer price mechanism from convention.

## Research disposition and missing work

Deliver a purpose-led evidence assessment for each material assumption: strongest support, independent counterevidence, reliability, unresolved alternatives and the decision implication using reviewed amounts or explicitly unquantified exposure. Prioritize missing research tasks by the conclusion they could change. Name the record or test needed, responsible capability, relevant population and condition for resolving the question. Restricted access remains an evidence limit, not evidence of no issue. Keep transaction research, domain calculation review and final investment approval distinct.
