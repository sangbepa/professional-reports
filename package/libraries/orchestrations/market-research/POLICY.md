# Market research planning policy — 0.1.0-dev.1

## market-research-rule-planning — Decision-led market inquiry

Fix product/service, customer segment, geography, channel, time period, currency, decision and information cutoff before collecting market sizes. Define units, revenue versus volume, gross/net value and which transactions belong in the market. Distinguish total market, serviceable market and obtainable demand; market size alone cannot establish achievable company revenue.

Plan independent top-down and bottom-up estimates where feasible: official statistics, original industry data, procurement/trade records and firm disclosures, with publication dates, coverage, sampling and reuse lineage. Repeated vendor quotations of the same original are not independent estimates. Map overlaps, missing segments and incompatible definitions before aggregation. Keep historical observations, estimates and forecasts separate; do not invent survey respondents, interviews, growth rates or paid-source access.

Plan demand, willingness-to-pay, adoption constraints, substitutes, competitors, distribution and unit-economics inquiries that can change the decision. Distinguish signed demand, intentions and analyst scenarios. Seek contrary evidence and sensitivity to market definition, coverage and pricing. Explain unresolved ranges instead of averaging incompatible estimates.

Return market-research-research-plan and market-research-access-and-capability-gaps, with source requests, testable questions, definitions, planned sampling/triangulation, owners and conclusion restrictions. Source/judgment roles review the inquiry and evidence feasibility, not an already-researched market conclusion.

## market-research-policy-production-block — Planning-only boundary

execution_support is planning-only; production_quality_verified is false. Report-mode execution is blocked. No completed market study, primary survey, verified dataset, forecast or recommendation is supplied by this profile. Actual research execution needs supported acquisition/analysis capabilities and a future domain execution contract.

Component refs are exactly {id, version, sha256}; requires uses string IDs. Stable domain-prefixed IDs remain stable across revisions and are never recycled. Main owns meta-orchestrator, codex-native and protected evaluations. The unconditional rule requires plan coverage only. Missing access is an unknown, never fabricated research or evidence of zero demand.
