# Investment memo planning policy — 0.1.0-dev.1

## investment-memo-rule-planning — Decision and thesis inquiry

Establish decision owner, instrument, ownership/rights, date/cutoff, currency, holding horizon, supplied constraints and actual access. Separate an investment proposal, an analyst recommendation and a documented approval. Do not invent risk appetite, investor suitability, authorization or a committee decision.

Plan evidence tests for the thesis, business economics, management/capital allocation, balance-sheet financing, valuation, catalysts, downside, liquidity and exit. Every thesis pillar needs a falsifiable question, strongest support, credible counterevidence and a condition that would change the decision. A catalyst has a mechanism and uncertainty; a date on a slide is not evidence it will occur.

Request an independently reviewed valuation handoff with its mandate, ownership basis, cutoff, methods, assumptions, source/model bindings and unresolved limits. Do not select fixed DCF/comps or manufacture a price target inside prose. Distinguish price from value, business risks from instrument rights, and conditional scenarios from probability-weighted forecasts. Quantification requires actual underlying analysis and explicit probability evidence if probabilities are used.

Plan downside financing/dilution, covenants, legal rights, liquidity and scenario consequences without asserting professional legal conclusions. Track contrary evidence and monitoring conditions. Return investment-memo-research-plan and investment-memo-access-and-capability-gaps with planned arguments, inquiry owners, dependencies and decision restrictions. Source/judgment roles cover the plan, not actual investment approval.

## investment-memo-policy-production-block — Planning-only boundary

execution_support is planning-only; production_quality_verified is false. Report-mode execution is blocked. A supported valuation domain alone does not complete this investment memo domain or establish authority to recommend, approve or transact. No investment committee approval, suitability assessment, execution or final memo is supplied. Future execution requires a versioned supported domain contract and actual analysis/review.

Use exact {id, version, sha256} component refs and string requires IDs. Keep readable investment-memo-prefixed internal IDs stable and never recycle retired IDs. Main owns meta-orchestrator, codex-native and protected evaluations. The unconditional rule checks coverage of planned capabilities/roles/output names only; no gate closes from its presence.
