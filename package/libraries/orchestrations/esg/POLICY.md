# ESG planning policy — 0.1.0-dev.1

## esg-rule-planning — Purpose, boundaries and measurement inquiry

Distinguish disclosure research, transaction/investment diligence and assurance readiness. Establish entities, sites, value chain, period, information cutoff, selected framework, jurisdiction and actual source access. Plan authoritative verification of framework applicability; publication of an international standard does not establish local legal obligations. Keep financial materiality, impact materiality and any combined approach explicit.

Plan tests of material environmental, workforce, supply-chain and governance claims. Policies, targets and ratings evidence statements, not implementation. Seek operating records, meters, invoices, incident logs, permits, workforce/supplier data and controls. Reconcile organizational/operational boundaries, ownership/control, acquisitions/disposals, exclusions and base-year changes. Separate absolute metrics from intensity and verify denominators.

For emissions inquiry, distinguish Scopes 1/2/3 and value-chain categories, location-based versus market-based Scope 2, and evidence for contractual instruments. Keep gross inventory, offsets, removals and avoided emissions separate. Domain specialists must supply actual measurement methods and uncertainty; no invented emission factor, ESG premium, fine or valuation adjustment.

Seek adverse records and contrary evidence, preserving allegation versus corroborated finding. Absence of public incidents is not evidence of no incidents. Link exposures to possible business consequences using reviewed quantification or explicitly unquantified inquiry. Return esg-research-plan and esg-access-and-capability-gaps. Plan source/judgment reviews without claiming completed measurement or assurance.

## esg-policy-production-block — Planning-only boundary

execution_support is planning-only; production_quality_verified is false. Report-mode execution is blocked. This component does not supply an ESG calculation engine, compliance opinion, disclosure renderer or assurance engagement. Readiness is a proposed assessment of traceability/control gaps, not an assurance conclusion. Future execution needs supported methods/tools, verified applicability and actual reviews.

Exact component refs are {id, version, sha256}; requires is string IDs. Use stable esg-prefixed internal IDs, never recycle them. Main owns meta-orchestrator, codex-native and protected evaluations. The unconditional rule checks plan capability/role/output coverage only; all returned gates begin pending or explicitly blocked.

Adapted from repository-relative valuation-workbench/skills/financial-report-research/references/esg.md and report-frame/SKILL.md; verify current standards during any actual engagement.
